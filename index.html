Dual-Audience System • Guest & Volunteer UX

Visitor Registration & Connect Team Portal

An end-to-end guest assimilation platform connecting first-time visitor registration on Sunday mornings with a streamlined follow-up portal for the Connect Team.

The Problem: Paper connect cards created manual data-entry delays, causing first-time guests to slip through the cracks between Sunday service and mid-week follow-up.

Systems Thinking: Designed two tailored views within one unified application—a friction-free mobile registration flow for guests and an actionable status dashboard for Connect Team volunteers.

IT/Media Ticket Submission & Technician Portal

A custom incident-reporting and triage workflow built for church staff and A/V technicians to report, prioritize, and resolve hardware, network, and media issues.

The Problem: Verbal requests and scattered text messages during busy weekend services led to missed repairs and unclear issue ownership across facilities and A/V teams.

Design Approach: Built a guided intake form that captures essential diagnostic context upfront, paired with a dedicated Technician Portal featuring clear status states and priority queues.

Kids Church Check-In & Sunday Operations App

A high-clarity check-in workflow designed to simplify Sunday-morning family check-ins and volunteer coordination under peak lobby traffic.

The Problem: Peak pre-service arrival windows required fast, error-proof lookups for parents and volunteers without overwhelming first-time families.

Design Approach: Focused on scannable typography, step-by-step confirmation states, and high-contrast touch targets tailored for tablet and mobile screens.
